# Code of conduct

We want a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone, whatever their background, language or experience.

## Expected

- Be kind, patient and constructive. Review the work, not the person.
- Use inclusive language. Respect Telugu and English speakers equally.
- Assume good intent and ask when unsure.
- Protect people's privacy. Never share personal data, locations or secrets.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, or sharing others' private information.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the LEAD through GitHub. A dedicated contact address is not set yet (OQ-005). Reports are handled in confidence and good-faith reporters are protected from retaliation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may restrict participation, to protect the community.
