# Pushkara Mitra delivery language

This glossary distinguishes project scope and decision gates so work can be approved and reported accurately.

## Language

**LEAD**:
The accountable human who selects project scope and authorizes work. A LEAD instruction does not supply another reviewer's independent decision.

**G0**:
The foundation adoption gate for authoritative standards, repository governance and accepted scope.

**G1**:
The foundation contract gate for identity, permissions, sessions, privacy, retention, consent and operational security decisions.

**Foundation tranche**:
The first approved delivery slice that establishes the capabilities on which later product journeys depend.
_Avoid_: Full product completion.

**Full product target**:
The complete original Pushkara Mitra experience represented by the retained functional acceptance inventory, including features awaiting their applicable approval gates.
_Avoid_: Foundation-only product.
