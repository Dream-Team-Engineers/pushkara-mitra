## The short version

You can use most of the app without signing in. If you sign in with Google, if you join a group, apply as a partner or write to us, we keep the small amount of information described below. We never store where you are.

## What stays on your phone only

Your language, light or dark theme, colour choice and starred (favourite) ghats are saved in your browser on your phone. They are never sent to us and are removed when you clear this app's site data. If you choose "remember me", a small signed cookie with your name and email stays on your device for up to 7 days or until you sign out.

## Your first name in a group

Other members of your group see your first name on the Group and Map screens, including when you are offline. It is never shown outside your group.

## Your position

Your position is shared only with your group, only while the Group or Map screen is open, and only if you agreed. It is held in memory while the group room is open and is never written to a database, file or log. The first time, sharing starts only after you tick that you agree and switch it on, and your agreement is recorded. After that, sharing starts when you open the Group or Map screen; your browser still asks for permission, and you can switch it off on the screen at any time. Members who share appear as pins with their first names. Pins for ghats and places are the same for everyone and are not about you.

## Contact us

Contact us helps you write an email to dreamteamtech@zohomail.in in your own email app. The app does not send or store what you type. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself. Please do not put medical details or details about children in it.

## Reports from volunteers

Crowd, river level and free food reports are posted by approved volunteers and priests. They are labelled "From a volunteer" with their age, and are not official figures. For river and flood warnings, follow the official bulletin and local officials. [Counsel view on liability: NEEDS REVIEW, OQ-045.]

## Where your data is held

Account data is held with Cloudflare, in its Asia-Pacific region. Cloudflare does not guarantee that data stays in India. [Counsel to confirm the position and any wording on transfers: NEEDS REVIEW, OQ-026.]

## If you are under 18

You are asked to say that a parent or guardian agrees. This is a declaration only; we are taking legal advice on whether stronger checks are needed. Do not sign in without that permission. Partners must be 18 or older. [NEEDS REVIEW, OQ-030.]

## Your choices

You can use the app without signing in. You can sign out and delete your account and data at any time on the "Delete my account and data" screen. You can remove the app's access in your Google Account settings. To ask about, correct or delete anything else, write to the address above. [Rights and complaint route wording: NEEDS REVIEW.]
