# Copyright and asset policy

Status: draft for counsel review.

1. **Nothing is added without a recorded right to use it.** Every image, icon, font, photograph, audio clip, map tile, text passage and translation needs a row in [asset-permissions.md](asset-permissions.md) before it is merged.
2. **Acceptable sources:** created by Dream Team Engineers (with contributor assignment), public-domain with proof, or licensed under a permissive licence on the allow-list in [licence-register.md](licence-register.md), or written permission from the rights holder kept on file.
3. **Never copied from** the official Akhanda Godavari Pushkaralu-2027 site, government portals, news sites, temple trusts or social media without written permission.
4. **No government emblems, logos or lookalike designs.** The app must never look official ([government-disclaimer.md](government-disclaimer.md)).
5. **Religious texts, images of deities and temple photographs** may have publisher or photographer rights even when the underlying text is old. Treat each as unverified until cleared.
6. **Attribution** obligations are recorded in [third-party-attributions.md](third-party-attributions.md) and shipped in the app's About screen.
7. **Dependencies:** the allow-list is enforced in CI. A new licence type needs an `ip-licence-review` issue.
8. **Takedown:** a rights holder complaint is handled as a P0 within one working day by LEAD.
