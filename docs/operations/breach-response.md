# Personal data breach response

Status: skeleton. Legal notification duties and deadlines are unverified (see [dpdp-checklist](../compliance/dpdp-checklist.md) DPDP-05 and [cert-in-checklist](../compliance/cert-in-checklist.md)). Counsel decides who is notified and what is said.

## Steps

1. Follow [incident-response](../security/incident-response.md) to declare and contain.
2. **Within the first hour:** record what data, whose data, how many people, when it started, and whether it is still happening.
3. **Preserve evidence** without copying personal data into more places.
4. **Counsel call:** decide notification to the Data Protection Board, affected people and CERT-In, with the deadlines they confirm.
5. **Notify affected people** in Telugu and English: what happened, what data, what we have done, what they should do, who to contact.
6. **Remediate and verify,** then update the threat model and data inventory.
